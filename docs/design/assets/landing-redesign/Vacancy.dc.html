Senior ML Engineer

Senior Full-time Remote · EU 4+ hrs overlap CET

We’re looking for a senior ML engineer to own the serving side of our clients’ AI products — the layer where models meet real traffic. You’ll ship inference systems that stay fast and affordable under load, and you’ll do it as the senior voice in a small pod.

What you’ll do

  • Design and operate low-latency model serving for vision and language workloads.
  • Own the path from notebook to production: batching, caching, autoscaling, observability.
  • Profile and cut inference cost without trading away accuracy or reliability.
  • Set the technical direction for a pod and mentor through code, not slideware.

What we’re looking for

  • 5+ years building backend or ML systems, with real production ML serving experience.
  • Fluency in Python and one of PyTorch / TensorFlow; comfortable with CUDA basics.
  • Strong instincts for latency, throughput and cost in distributed systems.
  • You’ve owned something end to end and can explain the trade-offs you made.

Nice to have

  • Experience with Triton, TorchServe, Ray, or a home-grown serving stack.
  • Kubernetes and GPU scheduling in anger.
  • A track record of open-source or writing that shows how you think.

What we offer

  • Senior-only pods, no bureaucracy, and genuine ownership of the outcome.
  • Remote-first with 4+ hours of CET overlap; async by default.
  • Competitive compensation, paid learning budget, and real time off.
