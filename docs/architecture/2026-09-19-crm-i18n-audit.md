# CRM text audit before internationalization (stage 1 of the i18n spec)

**Date:** 2026-09-19. **Basis:** backlog item 136 ("CRM text audit before multilingual support") and
workflow 8 ("Language / locale-leak sweep") — stage 1 of the spec
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-19-crm-i18n-design.md`. **Method:** seven read-only slices, each a
separate `copy-reviewer` agent over `origin/main` (`3990a584`); counts obtained by commands
(`git grep -c`), findings numbered `COPY-<SEV>-<slice>-<N>`, coordinates given as path and symbol, without
line numbers (`doc-durability.md`). Full slice reports are in the appendices below, verbatim.

**Result:** 156 findings (HIGH 43 · MED 78 · LOW 35) across seven slices. This is not a list of "someday" fixes:
§2 is what must be fixed **before** string extraction, otherwise the defect multiplies across two languages
or the translation breaks behavior.

## 1. Cross-cutting themes (recur in ≥3 slices)

1. **Server text is printed to the user verbatim and takes priority over the client's.**
   `extractBackendMessage` (`apps/web/app/lib/axios-utils.ts`) returns any `response.data.message`;
   that is how 214 English exceptions, codes like `CONTRACT_NOT_EDITABLE`,
   `'Invalid request body'` from `ZodExceptionFilter` and the raw axios `e.message` in seven dialogs
   already reach the screen today. Confirms the spec's decision §4.3 (codes + translation on the client) and
   makes it a precondition for everything else: while the server has priority, the client's error dictionary
   is only half the work.
2. **Text is frozen at module import.** 19 containers in `web-core`, 19 `Record` maps in
   `web-people`, 21 in `web-projects`, 9 in `shared` (67 strings), 96 Zod messages — computed before
   locale activation. Under Lingui all of them must become `msg` descriptors with `i18n._()` at the point
   of display; otherwise switching languages does not take effect and will look like a locale bug.
3. **One concept — three to five words, and that will go into both catalogs as is.** The `ADMIN` role is named
   four ways across 11 copies of `ROLE_LABEL(S)` in `apps/web` (canon — `components/ui/role-select.tsx`);
   project membership — four ways («Состав/Команда/участник/Эффективный состав», two in `_Избегать_`);
   `CONTRACT` — «Договоры/Договор/Контракт»; `READY_TO_SIGN` — two wordings; «инвойс»
   against the glossary. Consolidate duplicates **before** extraction, otherwise the catalog will have as many keys
   as there are copies.
4. **The `CONTEXT.md` glossary is violated in meaning, not in form.** «Выплата» in the second, forbidden
   meaning across five type labels; «Накопленные выплаты компании» names the opposite direction of
   money movement; «Долг компании» with the hint «ваша задолженность перед компанией»; «Валидация выплат» over
   the validation queue for **income**; `DROP` as «Посредник» — a fourth synonym. The translation will cement
   the error in two languages.
5. **Date, number and sort formatting — three locales at once.** `toLocale*` with `ru-RU`,
   `en-US`, `uk-UA` and with no locale (61 calls), `date-fns/locale/ru`, `localeCompare(…, 'ru')` in
   documents (for Ukrainian it misplaces `і ї є ґ`), `fmtDate` defined twice with a different
   format. A single `format.ts` (spec §4.1) is a mandatory foundation step.
6. **Tests are nailed to Russian literals.** `api`: 59 spec files / 248 assertions; `web`: 24 unit +
   24 E2E in finance alone; `notification-email-copy.spec.ts` expresses copywriting rules via substrings
   (`startsWith('Запрос на')`). The spec's decision §8 (testid/role anchors, text from the `uk` catalog) must
   be applied in the same PR that migrates the module.
7. **The product is already trilingual without i18n.** Ukrainian strings inside Russian maps
   (`CONTRACT_VARIABLE_DESCRIPTIONS`, `placeholder="Сортування"`, «гіг-контракт»), the English interview
   kanban (6 of 9 columns), a Ukrainian contract with Russian substitutions `'не указано'`,
   Russian tax terms `НДС/НДФЛ/ЕСВ/ЕП` in the skills list instead of `ПДВ/ПДФО/ЄСВ/ЄП`.

## 2. What to fix BEFORE string extraction (carried into the stage 2 "Foundation" plan and the first PRs of stage 3)

Behavior the translation breaks silently:

- `PendingKindSection`: `data-testid` is assembled from the localized section heading, and
  `pending/index.tsx` uses it to move focus after an action. The selector must come from `kind`, not from the text
  (COPY-H-docs-1).
- `ContractTab.tsx` and `UserDialog.tsx` branch the empty state on the English prose of the server error
  (`includes('no active contract template')`, `includes('template')`); the model to follow — `SignContractStep`
  branches on a code. Switch to codes (COPY-H-ppl-1, api).
- `EXPENSE_CATEGORIES` (`finance/constants.ts`) are written to `transactions.receiver_label` as Russian
  strings and read back into the edit dialog. Store the category key, show it per locale;
  migrate the existing values (COPY-H-fin-1).
- `notifications.title`/`body` in the database for three types (`INVOICE_SIGN_REQUIRED`, `INVOICE_SIGNED`,
  `VACANCY_APPLICATION`) not registered in `NOTIFICATION_TITLES`: render by `type` + `data` (spec §4.4),
  add the types to the registry (shared COPY-H-1).
- `documents-filter-sort.ts`: `localeCompare(…, 'ru')` → locale from context (COPY-H-docs-2).
- Separate labels tuned to pixels (`STATUS_FILTER_LABELS`/`_MOBILE` in `projects/constants.ts`, two
  pairs in vacancies): Ukrainian is longer — the tuning will stop being correct; have a designer check at
  320 in both languages (web-projects).

Meaning defects (will carry into any language):

- Glossary: «Выплата» in the second meaning (5 labels), «Накопленные выплаты компании», «Долг
  компании»/hint, «Валидация выплат», «Посредник», «инвойс», «Завершён» next to «Восстановить»,
  «был удалён» on archiving.
- Empty states and errors without "what to do": «Нет данных», «Ошибка», «Неизвестная ошибка» in nine
  finance files while a ready model exists in `finance/cascade-preview.ts`; raw `e.message` in
  `upload-document-dialog` and `use-notification-preferences` bypassing `getApiErrorMessage`.
- Manual plural forms: «1 команд», «1 приходов», «5 начисления», «2 получателей»,
  «Минимум 1 символов» (`zodIssueRu`), «5 актив. проекта» — move to ICU `plural`.
- `resolveProjectApprovalCaption`: «от ${name}» in the nominative case while the fallbacks are in the genitive;
  `document-card`: «К транзакции #<id>», where id is `projectId`, and the link goes to the generic `/finance`.
- Duplicate role maps (11 declarations; the drop profile shows `DROP` in Latin), three copies of the document
  delete dialog, three `CATEGORY_LABELS_RU`.

Server infrastructure (stage 2):

- The request has no locale (`Accept-Language` — 0 mentions), the user has no column; `shared`
  executes on both sides — a **request locale** (errors) and a **recipient locale** (emails,
  notifications) are needed, not a global `i18n` singleton.
- The document language in contracts is determined by the heuristic "is there a pipe table in the body" —
  replace with an explicit field during the contract migration (out of this milestone's scope, recorded).

## 3. Migration order that follows from the audit

1. **Foundation (stage 2)**: request/recipient locale, error-code envelope, `format.ts`, the `msg` form
   for module-level containers, the canon of role and category maps (`role-select.tsx`,
   `NOTIFICATION_TITLES`), the test rule "text from the catalog".
2. **`web-core` + `shared`** first: `NAV_ITEMS`, `ERROR_MESSAGES`, `STATUS_MESSAGES`, months, roles;
   the error-code registry and Zod messages — without them no module can be translated in full.
3. **`web-people`** (extract `UserDialog.tsx` ~2,500 lines as a separate task), then
   **`web-projects`**, **`web-finance`** (first `EXPENSE_CATEGORIES` and `format-amount.ts`),
   **`web-docs-notify`** (first `data-testid` and `localeCompare`).
4. **`api`**: 289 Russian + 214 English exceptions → codes; emails; the invoice PDF. The contract — out of this milestone.

## 4. How to verify the audit was acted on

- Every §2 finding is closed either by a PR (id in the backlog/PR body) or by a line "not doing it, because" in
  the stage spec. Reconciliation — by arithmetic over the slices' `Findings:` (156).
- Before the first stage-3 PR in a module: `git grep` over map duplicates (`ROLE_LABEL`, `CATEGORY_LABELS`) returns
  one declaration; `git grep -n "includes('" apps/web/app` finds no branching on error text.

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **First the fork "who owns the error text"** (`axios-utils.ts`, COPY-H-core-2 + the last row of table B). While the server has priority, any error string extracted on the client is half a solution; afterward one dictionary is translated for both languages.
2. **Shared label maps: roles, transaction types, months, size units.** Collapse the five role maps, the two names for `SENIOR_INCOME` and the four month arrays into one source each **before** extraction — otherwise Lingui will cement the discrepancies in the catalogs and they will be translated as different phrases.
3. **Module constants (19 containers in 16 files)** — a mechanical but mandatory change of form: `msg` + `i18n._()` in the render. Do it before all other JSX: while a constant is computed at import, the language won't switch, and it looks like a locale bug rather than a matter of notation.
4. **Numeric forms and cases** (COPY-M-core-5, `ROLE_RU`, `roleGenitive`, `share-slider`) — rewrite phrases whole under `plural`/`select`, not by substituting words. The already-broken «1 команд» get fixed here too.
5. **Last — `ArchiveConfirmDialog.tsx`**: the slice's most expensive file (56 text lines, 9 of 16 JSX concatenations, cases in data, four branches with numbers). It makes sense to rewrite it to `<Trans>` entirely as one task, rather than spreading it across the shared passes.

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **First not translation, but two preconditions.** Reduce `ROLE_LABELS` to the already-existing canonical map `components/ui/role-select.tsx` (`COPY-H-ppl-3`, `-4`, `-5`) and switch `ContractTab`/`UserDialog` from English-error substrings to codes (`COPY-H-ppl-10`). Both are behavior changes: doing them after extraction means extracting knowingly incorrect strings.
2. **Onboarding (`components/onboarding/*`, 4 files)** — first. The smallest volume, the highest defect cost (it's a new employee's first screen), and it holds `COPY-H-ppl-2`, `-3`, `-6`, `-7`. It also lets you break in `<Trans>` with a nested link.
3. **The whole slice's empty states and errors** (`COPY-M-ppl-1…9`, `-14`) — in one pass, because they are fixed not one-by-one but by introducing a common template "what happened → what to do". Exemplars to copy are already in the slice: `resume/ResumeStatusPanel.failureHint` and `contract/ContractTab` (the no-template branch).
4. **`components/users/UserDialog.tsx` (≈2500 lines) — a separate task and last among the large ones.** It alone gives four of the five «Ошибка валидации данных», its own role map, Zod messages and a two-step wizard; mixing it with anything else in one PR is a guaranteed conflict with tests.
5. **Tests — in the same PR as the corresponding file, not "later".** 25 unit files search by product text; translating strings while leaving red tests for the next round is more expensive here than it seems — half of them check RBAC visibility, and "red by text" is indistinguishable from "red by permissions".

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **First the label maps** (`interviews/constants.ts`, `projects/constants.ts`, `vacancies/constants.ts`): the 10 `*_LABELS` give the biggest coverage per file, they have no interpolation, and it is here that COPY-H-proj-1 (English stages), COPY-H-proj-5 (гіг/гиг/крипта) and COPY-M-proj-7 (gender) must be closed at once. Fix the terms **before** extraction, not after: otherwise four synonyms for membership and three names for one field go into both catalogs.
2. **Then pluralization and cases** — `VacancyCard.tsx` (the hand-rolled pluralizer), `zodIssueRu`, `project-approval-caption.ts`. These are the only places in the slice that need `plural`/a phrase rebuild rather than a mechanical replacement; skip them on this step and they go into the catalog as concatenations and surface at translation.
3. **Large screens one at a time**, starting with `$projectId.tsx` (257 fragments, 5 HIGH/MED findings): one file — one PR, otherwise the diff review is unreadable. Then `projects/index.tsx`, `vacancies/*`, `legend.tsx` + `ProjectLegendSection.tsx` (these two share ~80% of the text — reduce to shared keys rather than translate twice), `interviews/*`, `project.tsx`.
4. **Dates and money — a separate task for the whole slice at once**, not inside the screen PRs: 15 `toLocale*` calls with three different locales plus two `date-fns/locale`. While they are scattered across files, any screen fix fixes them only halfway.
5. **Tests — the last step and one PR**: 10 unit + 14 E2E hold on Russian literals. Switch them to `data-testid`/a catalog key, not to new Russian text; otherwise the next wording change reddens CI again. Separately re-check the tab-width tuning (`STATUS_FILTER_LABELS*`) at 320/375/768/1024 — the previous tuning cost six rounds and two CI failures, and the language under it changes.

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **First not translation, but data and codes.** Move `EXPENSE_CATEGORIES` out of the DB (COPY-B, row 1) and switch the test anchors from Russian text to `data-testid` (48 files). Until this is done, any wording change reddens 112 assertions and hides real breakages.
2. **Fix behavior defects that will carry into any language:** COPY-H-fin-1/2 (glossary terms), COPY-H-fin-3 (raw `error.message`), COPY-M-fin-5/6/7/12/13/15 (messages without an action). This is copywriter work, not translator work, and doing it after translation costs twice as much.
3. **Reduce formatting to one layer before translation:** one `fmtDate`/`fmtMonth`/`fmtAmount` per locale, remove the shadow duplicates (`fmtDate` in `DropFinancePage`, `fmtUsdt` ×3), replace `date-fns/locale.ru` with the locale from the Lingui context. Coordinate with `web-core`, since the root is `lib/format-amount.ts`.
4. **Collect plural forms and concatenations into ICU:** `company-share.ts` → `pluralizeProjects`/`pluralizeIncomes`, the four ternaries in `stats.tsx`, the lead-in concatenation of `cascade-preview.ts`, the JSX fragments via `{' '}` (20 places). Before this step, extracting strings is pointless — the extractor will pull out scraps.
5. **And only now extraction:** first `constants.ts` (the `TYPE_LABELS`/`STATUS_LABELS` maps — read by all the slice's screens), then the dialogs in descending size (`PayoutPaymentForm`, `CreateTransactionDialog`, `CompanySharePayoutModal`), then `index.tsx` and `stats.tsx`, and last — `aria-label`/`title`/`placeholder` in a separate pass, because the extractor doesn't see them and a miss here is silent.

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **Before translation — decouple behavior from text.** Remove `data-testid` from localized headings
   (`PendingKindSection` + `focusSelectorsAfterActing`) and `localeCompare(…, 'ru')`. These are the only
   two places where translation breaks not the look but the work; both fixes are self-contained and checked by tests.
2. **Consolidate duplicates before extraction.** One category-label map instead of three; one string for the
   `READY_TO_SIGN` status; a shared delete-confirmation dialog instead of three copies in card/row/detail-dialog.
   Each duplicate that reaches the catalog is a separate identifier and a future translation discrepancy.
3. **Close the HIGH A-defects that will carry into any language** (COPY-H-docs-1, -2, -5, -6): the restore
   promise, the «К транзакции» label over a project identifier, «инвойс» instead of «счёт», English axios on a
   Russian screen. Translating them as is means multiplying the defect across two languages.
4. **Then Lingui mechanics by file, from dense to sparse:** `documents.tsx` (counter + `pluralize` +
   ternaries in empty states) → `document-detail-dialog.tsx` / `upload-document-dialog.tsx` →
   `PendingItemRow.tsx` (four variants of one phrase — a `select` candidate) → the bell and
   `NotificationSettingsTab` (after the `shared` slice translates the notification registry).
5. **Tests — the last step and one pass:** replace 79 unit text assertions with testid where element presence is
   checked, and keep text assertions only where the wording itself is checked. Exclude E2E fixtures with Russian
   file names from this replacement.

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **First the channel, then the strings.** Introduce an error code in the response body and make the client read
   it, not the text (`extractBackendMessage`). While the server message is a UI string, any translation on the
   server will be a second place where the copy lives. The scheme already works on the landing
   (`errorKindForStatus`) — carry it over, don't reinvent it.
2. **Under this change — the four client branches** (COPY-H-api-3, section B): keep the two by codes, translate the
   two by English prose (`'no active contract template'`, `'template'`) to codes **before** the texts change.
   Otherwise two empty states fall off silently.
3. **Fix the defects translation can't cure** — before translation, so as not to translate broken text:
   COPY-H-api-4 (the internal breakdown in a 400), COPY-H-api-5 (three role dictionaries), COPY-H-api-6/7
   (the RBAC matrix and «IOU/obligation» in the ledger), COPY-M-api-8 (gender), COPY-M-api-13 (a paragraph in a toast).
4. **Emails and documents — a separate task after the locale column.** Here the text leaves the
   CRM and the language choice is not made by the UI: a recipient locale is needed, a decision for the invitation
   (the address is not yet confirmed) and resolving the «invoice in Russian / contract in Ukrainian» discrepancy (COPY-M-api-9/10).
   Rewrite `notification-email-copy.ts` in whole sentences — delete the `projectPhrase` /
   `sharePhrase` fragments, otherwise the Ukrainian cases can't be assembled.
5. **The remaining ~600 literals — mechanically, module by module**, in order of visibility:
   `finance` → `projects`/`teams` → `documents`/`resumes` → `job-sourcing` (paused, goes last).
   Tests (59 spec + 69 e2e) are fixed in the same commit as the module; `notification-email-copy.spec.ts`
   requires not a string fix but a rewrite of the invariant to message identifiers.

### Recommended migration order within the slice

1. **First the decision on text in the DB** (COPY-H-shared-5, kind 1 in §B). While the three old notification types return `title`/`body` from table strings, any amount of literal-extraction work leaves this hole open, and it will surface only after the language is switched.
2. **Then — the locale mechanism on the server**, before the first `t()`. `shared` executes on both sides (the table in §Scope), so the choice "request locale for a 400 / addressee locale for a notification" determines the shape of all subsequent fixes. A mistake here is cheaper to make once than to rewrite 96 Zod messages twice.
3. **Then the whole Zod layer** (`money.ts` → `finance.ts` → `users.ts` → `payment-requisites.ts` → the rest): 96 messages, uniform mechanics, closing COPY-H-shared-2, -3, -6 and 22 duplicates along the way. Reduce duplicates to one id **before** extraction, not after.
4. **Label maps** (`NOTIFICATION_TITLES` and neighbors, `CONTRACT_VARIABLE_DESCRIPTIONS`, `ROLE_LABELS`, the cascade messages) — they are few, but each requires a move to `msg` descriptors; COPY-H-shared-1 and COPY-M-shared-8 get fixed here too.
5. **Last — the assembled phrases** `describeNotification`/`subjectPhrase`/`resolveEditCascade` and the truncation budgets: these are the only places that need not a substitution but a **rewrite of the whole phrase** for each language's cases, and it makes sense to do it once the rest of the catalog exists and it's clear how long the strings come out.
