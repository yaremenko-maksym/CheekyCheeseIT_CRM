Used here for embedding into server-generated invoice PDFs because pdf-lib's
standard fonts (Helvetica, Times, Courier) do not include glyphs for the
Cyrillic Unicode block (U+0400–U+04FF), and our invoices contain Russian text
("АКТ ВЫПОЛНЕННЫХ РАБОТ", "ВЫПЛАТА ЗАРПЛАТЫ", etc.).
