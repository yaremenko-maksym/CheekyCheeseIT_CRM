Chip makers report a record quarter

Demand for data-centre accelerators pushed revenue at the largest chip makers to a record this quarter, analysts said on Tuesday. Shipments of consumer devices stayed flat, while memory prices rose for the third quarter in a row.

Investors now watch whether new fabs come online on schedule next year, and how export rules change the order books of equipment suppliers.
