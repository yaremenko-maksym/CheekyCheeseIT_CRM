**Decoding of the terms** (for all 8 sections):

| Term                              | What it is in simple language                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **TOV**                           | Tovarystvo z obmezhenoiu vidpovidalnistiu — a Ukrainian analog of an LLC. A legal entity, separate from the founder.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Diia City**                     | A special tax regime for IT companies in Ukraine. Gives tax benefits + a special instrument, the "gig contract", for the team. ([Law No. 1667-IX](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1667-IX) dated 15.07.2021, the current edition is from 01.01.2025) |
| **Exit-capital tax**              | Podatok na vyvedenyi kapital — "a tax only at the moment of profit withdrawal". While the money is in the TOV (reinvest) — 0%. Paid only when you withdraw dividends or do certain operations. Rate **9%**.                                              |
| **Single tax**                    | Yedynyi podatok — the simplified system. For a TOV group 3 — 5% of turnover (income, not profit). Limit ₴10 091 049 per year in 2026.                                                                                                                    |
| **PIT**                           | Podatok na dokhody fizychnykh osib — personal income tax. For gig specialists and salaries in Diia City — **5%**. For salaries outside Diia City — **18%**.                                                                                              |
| **ML (military levy)**            | Viiskovyi zbir — a separate tax of 5% (raised from 1.5% in 2024). Paid on top of PIT.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **USC**                           | Yedynyi sotsialnyi vnesok — social contributions. A minimum of **22% of the minimum wage** = **₴1 902.34/month** for each gig specialist/worker in 2026 ([DPS](https://dn.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/962430.html), collection date: 2026-05-31)       |
| **Gig contract**                  | An instrument unique to Diia City. Not an employment contract, not a civil-law contract, not a FOP — a "hybrid". Gives the specialist social guarantees + the TOV low taxes. ([Art. 1 of Law No. 1667-IX](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1667-IX))  |
| **KEP**                           | Kvalifikovanyi elektronnyi pidpys (qualified electronic signature). Free via the [Diia](https://ca.diia.gov.ua/) app, needed to file documents in registers and to sign contracts.                                                                       |
| **Business-activity code (KVED)** | Klasyfikator vydiv ekonomichnoi diialnosti. For IT the main one is **62.01 "Computer programming"**.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Minimum wage**                  | For 2026 — **₴8 647/month** ([DPS](https://dn.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/962430.html), collection date: 2026-05-31). The USC minimum is calculated from it.                                                                                           |

**The main takeaway:** in a TOV Diia City you have 3 "channels" of withdrawing funds — gig remuneration (for the team and founder salaries), dividends (for founder/partner withdrawal of profit) and reinvest (0% tax while the money is inside). Most IT founders use a combination.

#### 2. Tax optimization math — on a conditional example

**Scenario:** the TOV received **₴5 000 000 of turnover** in a year, after all payments to the team (gig specialist salaries, operating expenses) the **net profit = ₴2 000 000**, you as a founder want to withdraw **all** the profit in hand. Let's compare 4 regimes.

##### Regime A — TOV general system (18% profit tax)

| Step                                                                                                                   | Calculation                         | Amount         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Net profit before taxes                                                                                                | —                                   | ₴2 000 000     |
| Profit tax 18% ([Art. 134 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17))                    | 2 000 000 × 18%                     | -₴360 000      |
| Profit after profit tax                                                                                                | 2 000 000 - 360 000                 | ₴1 640 000     |
| Dividends → PIT 5% (resident) ([Art. 167.5.4 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17)) | 1 640 000 × 5%                      | -₴82 000       |
| Dividends → ML 5% ([Art. 161 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17))                 | 1 640 000 × 5%                      | -₴82 000       |
| **In hand**                                                                                                            |                                     | **₴1 476 000** |
| **Effective tax rate**                                                                                                 | (2 000 000 − 1 476 000) / 2 000 000 | **26.2%**      |

##### Regime B — TOV single tax group 3 (5% of turnover, without VAT)

| Step                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Calculation                         | Amount         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Turnover                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | —                                   | ₴5 000 000     |
| Single tax 5% ([Art. 293.3 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17))                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | 5 000 000 × 5%                      | -₴250 000      |
| ML on single tax 1% ([Art. 161 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17))                                                                                                                                                                                                                | 5 000 000 × 1%                      | -₴50 000       |
| Net profit of the TOV after single tax                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | 2 000 000 - 250 000 - 50 000        | ₴1 700 000     |
| Dividends → PIT 9% (the preferential rate for TOV single-tax payers [Art. 167.5.4 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17), commentary [buhoblik.org.ua](https://www.buhoblik.org.ua/nalogi/nalog-na-doxody-fizicheskix-licz/2280-pdfo-z-dividendiv.html), collection date: 2026-05-31) | 1 700 000 × 9%                      | -₴153 000      |
| Dividends → ML 5%                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | 1 700 000 × 5%                      | -₴85 000       |
| **In hand**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |                                     | **₴1 462 000** |
| **Effective tax rate**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | (2 000 000 − 1 462 000) / 2 000 000 | **26.9%**      |

⚠️ **Note on Regime B:** at a turnover close to the limit of **₴10 091 049/year** ([WebSearch: bip.net.ua](https://bip.net.ua/articles/yedinij-podatok-dlya-fop-i-tov/), collection date: 2026-05-31) — exceeding it even by ₴1 = automatic loss of single-tax-payer status + transfer to the general system + penalties. At our target scale (₴50-90M/year) — does NOT work.

##### Regime C — TOV Diia City resident exit-capital tax 9% (recommended)

| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Net profit before taxes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | —                                   | ₴2 000 000     |
| While the profit is reinvested in the TOV                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 0% tax                              | **₴0**         |
| You decide to withdraw as dividends — exit-capital tax 9% at the moment of payment ([para. 137.10 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17), [para. 141.9-1 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://buhgalter911.com/uk/normativnaya-baza/nalogovyi-kodeks/rozdil-iii-podatok-1020940.html), collection date: 2026-05-31) | 2 000 000 × 9%                      | -₴180 000      |
| Profit to be paid after exit-capital tax                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | 2 000 000 - 180 000                 | ₴1 820 000     |
| Dividends → PIT 5% (the preferential rate for Diia City residents ([WebSearch: i.factor.ua](https://i.factor.ua/ukr/news/rezident-diya-siti-viplachuye-dividendi-u-2026-roci-yaka-stavka-pdfo-i-vijskovogo-zboru/), collection date: 2026-05-31)                                                                                                     | 1 820 000 × 5%                      | -₴91 000       |
| Dividends → ML 5%                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | 1 820 000 × 5%                      | -₴91 000       |
| **In hand**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                     | **₴1 638 000** |
| **Effective tax rate**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | (2 000 000 − 1 638 000) / 2 000 000 | **18.1%**      |

##### Regime D — TOV Diia City resident 18% (alternative)

Makes sense if **ALL** the profit is withdrawn constantly (then the deferral of the exit-capital tax gives no benefit) — but in this case Diia City still gives an advantage via gig contracts for the team.

| Step                           | Calculation     | Amount         |
| ------------------------------ | --------------- | -------------- |
| Profit                         | —               | ₴2 000 000     |
| Profit tax 18%                 | 2 000 000 × 18% | -₴360 000      |
| Dividends → PIT 5% (Diia City) | 1 640 000 × 5%  | -₴82 000       |
| Dividends → ML 5%              | 1 640 000 × 5%  | -₴82 000       |
| **In hand**                    |                 | **₴1 476 000** |
| **Effective tax rate**         |                 | **26.2%**      |

##### Summary table

| Regime                           | Tax on the entity                            | Tax on withdrawal | In hand        | Effective rate |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| A. TOV general 18%               | ₴360k                                        | ₴164k             | **₴1 476 000** | 26.2%          |
| B. TOV single tax 5%             | ₴300k                                        | ₴238k             | **₴1 462 000** | 26.9%          |
| C. **TOV Diia City exit-cap 9%** | **₴0** (while reinvest) → ₴180k (on payment) | ₴182k             | **₴1 638 000** | **18.1%**      |
| D. TOV Diia City 18%             | ₴360k                                        | ₴164k             | ₴1 476 000     | 26.2%          |

##### What this math means

1. **The exit-capital tax (Regime C) saves ₴162 000/year** vs a TOV general on our conditional example **only** when the whole profit is withdrawn. If you leave part as reinvest — the saving **sharply grows** (on the reinvest part the tax = 0% against 18% in Regime A).

2. **Single tax 5% (Regime B) is NOT more advantageous than the exit-capital tax even at small turnovers** — it seems that 5% < 9%, but this is 5% of **turnover**, and 9% of **profit**. On the condition "turnover 5M, profit 2M" — B and A give the same ~26%, while C gives 18%.

3. **Break-even (when B becomes comparable to C)** — only if your **profit = 40-50% of turnover** (which is unrealistic in outsource, usually profit is 25-35%). At an outsource-profile scale B always loses to C.

4. **The hard limit of single tax** — ₴10 091 049/year. Reached with 1-2 large clients. **At our target scale of 30-50 contributors — B does not work in principle** (see the previous consultation).

5. **The main advantage of the exit-capital tax is the tax deferral.** If out of ₴2M profit you withdraw now only ₴500k (for life expenses) and leave ₴1.5M inside the TOV for reinvest (for example, for CRM development/marketing/hiring new seniors):
   - **Regime A:** you pay ₴360k on EVERYTHING + ₴45k on the withdrawn 500k = **₴405k** of lost funds immediately
   - **Regime C:** you pay exit-capital tax only on 500k = 500k × 9% = ₴45k + dividend taxes on the individual ₴45k = **₴90k**. ₴1.5M remains working inside the TOV.
   - **Saving from the deferral: ₴315k/year** — this is money that can be reinvested.

**Verdict of section 2:** On the conditional example ₴5M turnover / ₴2M profit — the saving is **₴162k/year** on a full withdrawal, **₴315k/year+** with a reasonable reinvest strategy. The main advantage is not in the rate, but in the **flexibility of the timing** of the tax payment.

#### 3. Roadmap by phases — sequentially with timing

**Decisions that need to be made:**

- **The TOV name** — it must be unique (check via [usr.minjust.gov.ua](https://usr.minjust.gov.ua/) — this is **free**)

  - Renting a real office (if you are already planning it)

  - **Oschadbank** (a state bank, reliable)
  - **Universal Bank (monobank Business)** — the cheapest and most convenient online, but sometimes a long compliance for non-residents
  - **Raiffeisen Bank Aval** (RBI group, good for FX operations)
- You open **3 accounts:** UAH (the main one), USD, EUR (currency sub-accounts — usually opened in one package)

**An accountant (start outsource):**

- You immediately conclude a contract with an accounting company. Approaches:
  - **Full outsourcing** — ₴3 000-10 000/month for a small TOV (1-3 specialists, up to 30 operations/month) ([WebSearch: cba.net.ua/price](https://cba.net.ua/price/), collection date: 2026-05-31)
  - **Hourly work** — ₴200-800/hour ([WebSearch: cba.net.ua](https://cba.net.ua/price/), collection date: 2026-05-31)
- **I recommend full outsourcing** — do not try it yourself, the tax reporting is complex

**Timelines:**

- Bank opening — **2-7 days** (depends on the bank and the compliance check)
- A contract with an accountant — **1-3 days**

**Cost:**

- Opening a UAH account — **₴0** usually
- Opening FX accounts — **₴200-1000** one-off (depends on the bank)
- A setup fee at the accountant — **₴3 000-10 000** one-off (entry into the accounting system, preparation for the first reporting)
- **Total Phase 2: ₴3 000-11 000**

**Who does it:** the User themselves (the bank) + the accountant (their work).

##### Phase 3 — Filing the application to Diia City as a startup resident (Week 3-5)

**What is done in simple language:** you officially become a resident of Diia City in the "startup" mode — this is the preferential option when you do not yet have 9 people or €1200/month for each.

**Important:** the **startup mode** gives you a **24-month preferential period** — during this time you must reach the main criteria (9 specialists + €1200/month for each). If you do not — you lose residency.

**Documents that need to be prepared:**

- An extract from the Unified State Register (fresh, ≤30 days)
- The TOV Statute
- An application for acquiring resident status — generated via [diia.gov.ua/services/zayava-pro-nabuttya-statusu-rezidenta-diya-city](https://diia.gov.ua/services/zayava-pro-nabuttya-statusu-rezidenta-diya-city)
- A document on compliance with the startup conditions: you confirm that the company is registered **no earlier than 24 months** BEFORE filing (Phase 1 gives a clean start, ok)
- Confirmation that the company's income for the previous year does not exceed **1167 minimum wages (~₴10 091 049)** ([WebSearch: bip.net.ua/articles/diya-siti](https://bip.net.ua/articles/diya-siti-umovi-vimogi-perevagi-j-nedoliki/), collection date: 2026-05-31) — if the TOV has just been registered, income = 0, you pass automatically
- For a TOV startup: it is enough to confirm the qualifying business-activity codes 62.01 etc.

**Where to go:** online via [diia.gov.ua/services/zayava-pro-nabuttya-statusu-rezidenta-diya-city](https://diia.gov.ua/services/zayava-pro-nabuttya-statusu-rezidenta-diya-city).

**Timelines:**

- **10 working days** — the review term by the Ministry of Digital Transformation ([WebSearch: diia.gov.ua](https://diia.gov.ua/services/zayava-pro-nabuttya-statusu-rezidenta-diya-city), collection date: 2026-05-31)
- **The principle of tacit consent:** if there is no answer in 10 days — the status is considered obtained automatically
- Calendar-wise: **2-3 weeks** from filing to the actual start of work under Diia City

**Cost:**

- **₴0** of state duty and fees — Diia City is not a paid procedure ([WebSearch: city.diia.gov.ua](https://city.diia.gov.ua/), collection date: 2026-05-31)
- A lawyer's help with the filing (I recommend if the first time) — **₴5 000-15 000**
- **Total Phase 3: ₴0-15 000**

**Who does it:** the User themselves (the form is simple) or a lawyer (I recommend if the structure is non-standard — multiple founders, a non-resident founder).

##### Phase 4 — Transition to the exit-capital tax 9% (after obtaining resident status, Week 5-6)

**What is done in simple language:** now you are a Diia City resident, but your tax regime is by default **18% profit tax**. To switch to the exit-capital tax 9% — a separate procedure.

**When to do it:**

- **Immediately after obtaining resident status** — if you plan to reinvest and not withdraw dividends constantly (I recommend)
- **Or wait until the start of the next quarter** — because the transition to special conditions applies from the 1st of the quarter ([para. 137.10 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17), commentary [news.dtkt.ua](https://news.dtkt.ua/taxation/profits-tax/108705-opodatkuvannia-rezidentiv-diia-siti-u-2026-roci-tri-prakticni-situaciyi-tocka-zoru-dpsu-ta-komentar-redakciyi), collection date: 2026-05-31)

**Documents that need to be prepared:**

- An application to choose the special conditions of taxation (a form approved by the Ministry of Finance)
- The TOV accounting policy (if none — the accountant will compose it)

**Where to submit:** to the **DPS** at the place of the TOV's registration (electronically via the [Electronic Cabinet of the Payer](https://cabinet.tax.gov.ua/)).

**Timelines:**

- Filing — **15 working days** before the start of the quarter from which you want to apply the exit-capital tax
- Confirmation from the DPS — usually automatic upon fulfillment of the conditions

**Cost:**

- **₴0** of state duty
- The accountant's work on the arrangement — **already in the ongoing fee**
- A tax consultation before the transition (**I recommend**) — **₴5 000-15 000**
- **Total Phase 4: ₴0-15 000**

**Who does it:** the accountant + a tax consultant (I recommend for a review of the consequences).

##### Phase 5 — Gig contracts with the team + a Services Agreement with clients (Week 6-10)

**What is done in simple language:** you arrange the team via gig contracts (and not via the old FOPs), prepare the contract package for clients with the correct multi-payment clause (bank-only from the previous consultation).

**Documents that need to be prepared:**

**Gig contracts with the team:**

- A gig-contract template (~5-15 pages) — content:
  - Parties: the TOV Diia City resident + the gig specialist
  - A description of the services (detailed — otherwise the DPS may reclassify it into an employment one)
  - Remuneration (a fixed amount or a formula + the structure: the base remuneration + surcharges)
  - The duration of the contract (fixed-term or indefinite)
  - The right to sick leave, vacations (the norm of Art. 21-24 of Law No. 1667-IX)
  - IP rights — critical, the transfer of the results of the work to the TOV
  - NDA + non-compete (if needed)
  - Termination — the procedure
  - Requisites of the parties
- **Mandatory:** the gig specialist's tax ID + place of residence + a USDT wallet / bank card (for payouts)

**A Services Agreement with the client (US/EU/UA):**

- Use the recommendations of Q2.4 from the previous consultation ([2026-05-31-tov-multi-channel-revenue.md](2026-05-31-tov-multi-channel-revenue.md)) — a bank-only payment clause, a single-issuer clause, an invoice format clause

**Where to submit the gig contracts:**

- Signed by both parties (a KEP or a wet signature)
- **Does NOT require registration** in any register
- Stored in the TOV's accounting + with the gig specialist
- Notification of the DPS about the gig specialists — via filing a **Tax Calculation (form 4DF)** in the standard procedure ([WebSearch: 7eminar.ua/news/5620](https://7eminar.ua/news/5620-rezident-diya-siti-vinagoroda-likarnyani-oplacuvana-pererva-gig-specialistam-ta), collection date: 2026-05-31)

**Timelines:**

- Development of the gig-contract template by a lawyer — **2-4 weeks**
- Signing with each gig specialist — **1-3 days per person** (including the KEP signature)
- A Services Agreement with the client — **depends on the client** (typically 1-4 weeks to agree)

**Cost:**

- Development of the gig-contract template by a lawyer — **₴15 000-30 000** one-off (then you use it for all specialists)
- Development of a Services Agreement template — **₴15 000-40 000** one-off (cross-border rates in EN + UA, multi-payment clauses)
- IP assignment + NDA addendums — **₴5 000-10 000** if separately
- **Total Phase 5: ₴35 000-80 000** one-off for the contract package (fully reusable)

**Who does it:** **A lawyer (corporate + IT contracts).** **Do not write the templates yourself** — mistakes in a gig contract can lead to reclassification into an employment one (and that is +18% PIT instead of 5%).

##### Phase 6 — CRM platform documents (Terms of Service + Privacy Policy) (Week 8-12, in parallel)

**What is done in simple language:** if the CRM platform will be used by external clients/specialists (not only internal) — legal documents are needed that govern the relationship with the users.

**Applicable if:**

- The specialists use the CRM through their account (their personal data is stored — phones, USDT wallets, passports)
- The clients log into the CRM (for example, to track the progress of their projects)

**If the CRM is strictly internal (only for the TOV's employees + the team's gig specialists):** Terms of Service are not mandatory, but a **Privacy Policy** is mandatory per [Art. 11 of Law No. 2297-VI "On the Protection of Personal Data"](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2297-17).

**Documents that need to be prepared:**

- **Terms of Service (ToS)** — bilingual UA/EN
- **A Privacy Policy** — UA + EN (if EU users are present — GDPR-compliant)
- **A Cookie Policy** (if the CRM is on the web)
- **A DPA (Data Processing Agreement)** template — for the S3 provider, the email service, any subprocessors
- An internal **Privacy Impact Assessment** — for wallet fields (sensitive financial data)

**Cost:**

- A full package from a specialized IT lawyer — **₴30 000-80 000**
- A minimal package from a general corporate lawyer — **₴15 000-30 000** (but the quality is weaker, does not cover GDPR specifics)
- **Total Phase 6: ₴15 000-80 000**

**Who does it:** **An IT/privacy lawyer.** A general corporate lawyer is not enough for GDPR.

#### 7. Possible mistakes that are often made

##### Top 10 mistakes in setting up a TOV Diia City

1. **Registering the TOV immediately on single tax 3% or 5%** — forgot that Diia City requires the general system. Then you have to transition — time, nerves are lost.

2. **A gig-contract template from the internet without a lawyer** — often reclassified into an employment one at the first DPS audit → +18% PIT + penalties + USC overpayment for 3 years back. Standard red flags: a fixed schedule, control of working hours, duties "like a staff worker".

3. **Filing to Diia City without fulfilling the 24-month window for a startup** — if the TOV exists > 24 months and the turnovers > ₴10M — you cannot as a startup, you need to be a full resident immediately (9 specialists + €1200). Companies often miss this window due to a registration delay.

4. **Not monitoring the Diia City criteria** — the company thinks that after obtaining the status nothing needs to be done. In reality you need a quarterly self-check + an annual compliance report. Otherwise you lose the status.

5. **Paying gig remuneration below €1200/month under full residency** — an automatic trigger for applying 18% PIT (instead of 5%) for the **WHOLE** company in that quarter ([WebSearch: 1tax.com.ua](https://www.1tax.com.ua/base/local-tax/local-state-tax-service/dia-city/), collection date: 2026-05-31).

6. **A "founder's loan" as a way to withdraw money without taxes** — formally OK if correctly arranged (a loan agreement + a repayment schedule), but **systematic use without real economic substance** = the DPS reclassifies it into dividends + exit-capital tax + a 25% fine + late fees.

7. **The absence of an accounting policy** — mandatory on the general system from the first day. If the accountant did not compose it — the DPS may refuse any expenses during an audit.

8. **The same FOP for cross-border clients and for one's own gig contracts** — a conflict of interests, a potential reclassification into employment + 18% PIT.

9. **Outdated Services Agreement templates for US/EU clients** — the applicable law is not specified, dispute resolution is not specified, there is no IP assignment clause. At the first dispute with a client — a catastrophe.

10. **Using a Privacy Policy from GitHub instead of a custom-made one** — GDPR fines up to 4% of global turnover (for US/EU clients in your CRM may be triggering). Generic templates do not cover specific data flows (USDT wallets = financial data).

##### Where to save laughingly small amounts and lose a lot

- **Filing to Diia City on your own without legal help** for a complex structure (a non-resident partner) — a saving of ₴10-15k, a loss: refiling in 1-2 months, a missed tax period (= 1 quarter of exit-capital-tax deferral).

- **A cheap accountant "for ₴2000/month"** — a saving of ₴5-15k/month at the start, a loss: a missed declaration = a fine of ₴5-10k + a tax overpayment due to misunderstanding the exit-capital-tax nuances (+ ₴50-200k/year).

- **A Model Statute when a custom one is needed (a multi-founder structure)** — a saving of ₴10-15k, a loss: the impossibility of vesting (a founder left in the second month with 30% of the company = a legal nightmare).

- **A gig-contract template downloaded from the internet** — a saving of ₴15-30k, a loss: reclassification = +13% PIT × ₴100k+ a month on 10 specialists = ₴130k/month × 12 = **₴1.5M/year** + penalties.

- **"We'll do the reporting ourselves later"** — a saving of 2-3 years of accounting fees, a loss: at the first tax audit the reconstruction of bookkeeping after the fact = ₴100-500k one-off + penalties.

#### 8. Tax savings tactics within LEGITIMATE bounds

**The principles to be guided by** (these are not "schemes", this is normal tax planning that is allowed by the Tax Code):

##### Tactic 1 — The optimal moment of entering Diia City

- **Apply as soon as you register** — do not wait "until the turnovers come". The startup mode gives you 24 months of a preferential period without the obligation of 9 specialists and €1200/month.
- **Transition to the exit-capital tax immediately after obtaining the status** (if you plan to reinvest), not in the next quarter — for every month of waiting you lose the tax benefit on reinvest.
- **A combo of Diia City startup + a FOP for the founders in parallel** — an individual can be a gig specialist in the TOV + a FOP in another activity simultaneously ([WebSearch: news.dtkt.ua/simple/individual-single-tax/88330](https://news.dtkt.ua/simple/individual-single-tax/88330-ci-moze-fizosoba-jedinnik-iii-grupi-uklasti-gig-kontrakt-na-vikonannia-robit-z-rezidentom-diia-siti), collection date: 2026-05-31). This gives flexibility for personal flows.

##### Tactic 2 — Distribution structures (salary vs dividends)

| Source of income                  | Taxes at the moment of payment                                 | When it is more advantageous                 |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Gig remuneration / salary         | 5% PIT + 5% ML + ₴1902/month USC = ~10% + fixed cost           | For the main fixed income (living, expenses) |
| Dividends of a Diia City resident | 9% exit-capital tax (TOV) + 5% PIT + 5% ML (individual) = ~19% | For a large withdrawal of profit             |
| Reinvest (not withdraw)           | 0%                                                             | When you plan growth / new products          |

**The strategy for you:**

- **Gig remuneration for living expenses** — €1200-2000/month (closes the minimum requirement + covers the personal cash flow), effective rate ~10%
- **Reinvest 70-80% of the profit** — on CRM development, hiring, marketing, runway
- **Dividends once in 1-2 years** on large purchases / investments outside the business — effective rate ~19% **only at the moment of payment**

##### Tactic 3 — The benefit on dividends for "undistributed periods"

[Art. 167.5.1 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17) + commentary ([WebSearch: i.factor.ua](https://i.factor.ua/ukr/journals/nibu/2026/march/issue-21/article-136682.html), collection date: 2026-05-31): **if a Diia City resident did not pay dividends for the last 2 calendar years in a row — the next payment may be exempt from PIT** (but not from ML).

**Application:**

- You enter Diia City, do not withdraw dividends 2024+2025+2026 = only reinvest and gig remuneration
- In 2027 you make a large dividend payment — a saving of 5% PIT × amount
- On a ₴2M payment = **₴100 000 of savings one-off**

⚠️ **Caveat:** the rule is interpreted strictly by the DPS (a calendar year = 1 January - 31 December). Precise documentation that there were no payments is needed. **A tax consultant is mandatory** for this tactic.

##### Tactic 4 — USC minimization via the gig structure

- USC for gig specialists: **22% of the minimum wage** (₴1 902.34/month) regardless of the real remuneration
- USC for employment workers: **22% of the real salary** (if the salary is above the minimum wage)

**Example:** a SENIOR with a remuneration of ₴100 000/month:

- As a gig specialist: USC = ₴1 902 → the load on the TOV = ₴1 902/month
- As an employment worker: USC = ₴22 000 → the load on the TOV = ₴22 000/month
- **Saving = ₴20 000/month × 12 = ₴240 000/year per SENIOR**

This is a legitimate difference of regimes, **not a scheme**. It is laid into Law No. 1667-IX as the main incentive of Diia City.

##### Tactic 5 — Tax-deductible expenses in the exit-capital tax

Although the exit-capital tax is not calculated from the profit (but only at the moment of withdrawal), there are operations that **create an object of the exit-capital tax** even without formal dividends ([para. 137.10 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://buhgalter911.com/uk/normativnaya-baza/nalogovyi-kodeks/rozdil-iii-podatok-1020940.html), commentary [news.dtkt.ua/taxation/profits-tax/108705](https://news.dtkt.ua/taxation/profits-tax/108705-opodatkuvannia-rezidentiv-diia-siti-u-2026-roci-tri-prakticni-situaciyi-tocka-zoru-dpsu-ta-komentar-redakciyi), collection date: 2026-05-31):

- Payments to non-residents without a CCT (Convention on Corporate Tax) — fall into the exit-capital-tax base
- A gratuitous transfer of assets — falls in
- A repayable financial aid to non-residents — falls in
- Expenses > ordinary prices with related parties — fall in

**Tactics:**

- **Make payments to non-residents via a bank wire** (and not via crypto) with a correctly arranged contract — it falls into the standard tax process, does not trigger the exit-capital tax
- **Do not give a "loan from the TOV"** to partners / related persons without a commercial purpose
- **Document ordinary prices** for all deals with related parties (this is [Art. 39 of the Tax Code of Ukraine](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17) — transfer pricing, required at large turnovers)

##### Tactic 6 — Competent planning of payments over time

- **Do not pay dividends in December** — it falls into the annual reporting with a large tax event. Better January of the next year.
- **Plan large expenses toward the end of the quarter** — the exit-capital tax is optimized for the next quarter (if the expense withdraws capital).
- **A consolidated payout schedule** — once a quarter, not every month. Fewer operational costs, simpler documentation.

##### Tactic 7 — What I do **NOT recommend** (on the edge of schemes)

These tactics exist, but **the line between optimization and evasion here is blurred**. If there is a temptation — **mandatory via a tax consultant**, otherwise the risk of Art. 212 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine:

- **A "founder's loan" as a way to withdraw profit with a repayment later** — formally OK if there is real economic substance, but systematic use = the DPS reclassifies it
- **Purchases of services from "phantom" FOPs** to increase the TOV's expenses — a classic scheme, easily detected
- **Subcontracting via an offshore** (Cyprus, Estonia) for cross-border clients — legitimate only with real substance on the offshore (office, employees, director)
- **"Splitting" one client into several contracts** to stay under the single-tax limit — a direct scheme, **do not do it**

**The rule:** if a tactic gives a saving of > 15% of the effective rate — this is **most likely a scheme**, not optimization. All "real" tactics give ±2-5% of the effective rate.

### Recommendation (best for business)

**The final numbered list — what to do specifically along the timeline:**

#### Month 1 (weeks 1-4) — Setup foundation

1. **Week 1 (Preparation):**
   - Get a KEP via the Diia app (free, 1 hour of work)
   - Make decisions on the structure (name, legal address, founders, shares, business-activity codes)
   - If there is a partner — find a corporate lawyer and sign a Founders' Agreement (₴15-40k)
   - Prepare the founders' documents

2. **Week 2 (TOV registration):**
   - File via [Diia](https://diia.gov.ua/services/reyestraciya-tov-na-pidstavi-modelnogo-statutu) or via a legal firm
   - Open bank accounts (UAH + USD + EUR)
   - Get an extract from the Unified State Register

3. **Week 3 (Accountant + first filing):**
   - Conclude a contract with an accounting company (₴8-20k/month)
   - Setup of the accounting system + the accounting policy
   - File the application to Diia City as a startup resident

4. **Week 4 (Compliance setup):**
   - Wait for Diia City resident status (10 working days + buffer)
   - Prepare the documents for the transition to the exit-capital tax
   - A tax consultation on the exit-capital-tax strategy (₴5-15k)

#### Month 2 (weeks 5-8) — Operations launch

5. **Week 5-6 (Transition to the exit-capital tax):**
   - File the application to the DPS on choosing the exit-capital tax
   - In parallel: the corporate lawyer develops a gig-contract template + a Services Agreement template (₴30-70k)

6. **Week 6-7 (First signing of the team):**
   - Sign gig contracts with the first 2-3 specialists (founder + 1-2 SENIORs)
   - Start generating the first invoices to clients
   - The banking compliance check passes for the first client

7. **Week 7-8 (First operations):**
   - The first receipts of funds to the TOV accounts
   - The accountant does the first month's reporting
   - An internal review of the structure — what works, what needs to be adjusted

#### Month 3 (weeks 9-12) — Growth setup

8. **Week 9-10 (Privacy + ToS):**
   - If the CRM is public — the privacy lawyer develops a Privacy Policy + ToS (₴30-80k)

9. **Week 10-12 (Compliance rhythm):**
   - The first quarterly declaration (if it falls into this period)
   - A self-audit of the Diia City criteria
   - Preparation for scale (hiring more specialists)

#### Months 4-6 — Stabilization

10. Signing the rest of the specialists onto gig contracts
11. Establishing a quarterly compliance routine
12. A tax consultation on the Q1 results for adjustments
13. Preparation for the full-resident status (when you reach 9 specialists + €1200)

#### Months 7-12 — Optimization

14. Transition to the full Diia City resident (when the conditions are met)
15. Optional: moving the office to a Diia City cluster (if needed)
16. Tax planning on dividends (if you plan large payouts)
17. An audit of the financial reporting (if there is a non-resident partner)

**What to START DOING TOMORROW to kick off the process:**

1. **Install the Diia app + arrange a KEP** (1 hour, free)
2. **A list of 5 potential TOV names** + check the uniqueness on [usr.minjust.gov.ua](https://usr.minjust.gov.ua/) (30 min)

**Final thought:** you can start the process **tomorrow** without any material risks (the KEP is free, choosing a name is free, commercial offers are free). The first paid step is the Founders' Agreement (if there is a partner) or registration via a legal firm. Both items are inexpensive relative to the tax savings that the structure gives.

**Total setup budget realistically: ₴80-150k** in 8-10 weeks, after which ongoing ₴14-40k/month. On the conditional example of ₴2M profit — a saving of ₴162-315k/year relative to a TOV general. **ROI on the setup: < 12 months**, after that — pure savings.
